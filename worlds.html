Of Stars And Stories: A Concept

You wake up in a battlefield with so many wounds you should be dead already. Four whole months are missing from your memory. How did this happen and what next?

OSAS is an attempt at a serious exploration of something I keep seeing in anime: people isekaing into bodies of a different gender, and people not caring at all for the worlds they leave behind (doesn't matter which one it is). So, this story asks: What if Hailey hated this so much she left as soon as she could? What if, her strange actions came back to haunt the one she replaced?

Part of me trying to accomplish this is the honestly very stereotypical worldbuilding. I won't shy away from gore (actually there's a damn lot of gore in the plot), but as a whole it's medival fantasy, dnd vibes. We've got a kingdom and royalty and even love interests!! They're just also all people.

The thing that sets this world apart (if you don't count, like the entire plot) is the religion. Heavily inspired by the indie game In Stars and Time (psst can you tell), the people of Trecia worship the stars as deities. There is no supreme god, not when the universe is so large, but for Earth, there are two major deities. To quote the storyteller:

Mighty Heila blesses us all with warmth and light, from the bean-stalk in the garden to the story-teller who hearkens.

Great Polaris guides us home, from the sailors at sea to the children playing hide-and-seek.

The main character... isn't Hailey. She's the one who got reincarneted, don't get me wrong, but she's left the damn place in 4 months. No, Crown Prince Edward takes the lead.

The Aristocracy: A Hollow Knight AU

A trio (perhaps quintet) of time loopers have had enough of Hallownest. They move to Pharloom, because the plot awaits no-one, and set up an aristocracy. Shenanigans ensue.

Aka: Time Loop (but we all moved to Pharloom)
...yeah I just got a nicer name

This is the silly world. The crackfic location. But we do have feelings. Prepare for some inexplicable drabbles, I think.

I try to keep the magic system internally consistent, but things get cursed sometimes. This is, of course, more of an isekai loop than a time loop. I like the concept of isekai.
