“Hello there, little one.”

The sounds are very loud, and the colors very bright, but they look towards the voice. In front of them stands a figure colored like the sea, and another, smaller one colored like the sky. The figures look down at them, and the former turns away.

“Wait-” The latter starts.

“No.”

The lights close on them, and the figures vanish.

~

The lights don’t stay closed. That, or they get used to the darkness fast. The world around them is red and wild, the land littered with flames and torches stabbed into the ground.

Their world is small.

The red tapers off into pure darkness before their eyes stop registering any shapes.

They walk towards the darkness. 

It’s calm, but it’s moving, breathing, alive. It seems to say “hello” as they approach. It seems to promise, “You’ll return to us one day.”

~

They look outside their world, and it’s so, so different from what they had known. The earth is creased and uneven, a light shines from the sky, and the people - there’s so many of them - pray, pray to… them.

Why would they do that? They’re nothing special. Though, from what they… know, it’s not common to become aware without being born, or to grow and learn completely alone.

Maybe that makes them worth praying to?

But it doesn’t seem very nice, to be alone and away for what might just be eternity.

You’ll return to us one day

Or maybe not eternity, but those people seem to want them to stay around.

“O King of Nightmares, spare us our suffering and consume our demons. For any kindness we will pay, for any kindness we will serve.”

That doesn’t seem very nice to them, either. And, could they really do what the people asking for?

~

As it turns out, they can. They don’t know how, or why (except that they’ve been prayed to, that their actions have been asked for), but with simple intent, and instinct, they drive away and consume the flames littering their world.

The flames make them feel stronger. That probably means they should keep doing this. But they can’t take a flame that isn’t wanted gone, and there’s weirdly many of those.

Maybe, in time, they won’t need the permission of another.

~

“Hello there, little one”

It’s the sky-colored figure again. But this time, it’s not speaking to them.

A golden land with low-flying clouds and what seem to be stalks of wheat appeared recently. It blends into their red world, to the point where they might have become one. And they saw another figure in that land, too. Now, the sky and sea are speaking to it.

“Come with us,” the sea says, “Do not flee.”

Why? These gods would take a stranger with them, but not the one they seem to have actually waited for? Why would the new one want to go with them, anyways?

“Beside you is the realm owned by Fear, and you are Hope. He will kill you, little one, and anyone who gets in his way.”

That’s stupid. They run over to the new figure, and put out a hand. Whatever the sky and sea are doing, they don’t trust it.

It seems like the new person agrees. They take their hand and, together, run away from the blue gods.

~

Hope looks at their mysterious savior. Cloaked in black and red, and coming from the fire-filled land west.

“You’re Fear, aren’t you?”

“Maybe. I don’t know. The sky and sea said that, right? And who cares what they say.”

“That’s what they are? The sky and sea?”

“Probably. They look like it, don’t they?”

“I guess?”

Hope looks again. Fear seems to know so much more than they do. They know of the sky and sea, but never once would they have thought, “that’s what those figures are”.

“What now?”

Talks; the first of many

They stay with each other for a good while, but it’s not a long time in the grand scheme of things. They decide that, despite where it came from, Fear and Hope are good names. And, well, they need names, even if they don’t like them.

How else do you call for a friend?

~

They talk, a lot. 

Sometimes, their questions have easy answers.

“Hey, Fear, why are the bugs praying to me?”

“I think, because we can do what they want us to. Do you think we’re gods like the sky and sea?”

“Maybe. Probably? But I can’t make their crops grow and I can’t fight off the invasions that they don’t want to, even if I can give them hope and change their dreams.”

“They want a lot from you, don’t they?”

“Yeah.”

“Mine just want me to eat their nightmares. Maybe you can try telling them that you can’t do that much, and ask for something sometime.”

~

Other times, they have to figure it out themselves.

“What’s a ‘brother’?”

Hope had heard their moths saying that a lot about each other. Along with “mother”, “father”, and “sister”. It seems to mean a person of significance, but they feel like it’s more. More complicated, and more important.

“I think it’s someone who you share a parent with. I asked the butterflies, well, about ‘sisters’, and they said it’s the same thing.”

And that’s another word they keep hearing - “parent”. 

“What’s a parent, then?”

“I don’t really know. Someone important, I guess. I think they’re supposed to teach you, and help you when you’re a kid.”

“But the people who get called parents aren't always very nice, are they?”

“No, I guess not. But how would you find a parent otherwise?”

“Well, there's gotta be something else. Maybe I can ask!”

“Yeah. Oh, who do you think our parents are?”

“You think they're the same people for both of us, Fear?”

“Oh, maybe? But I wonder if it would be the sky and sea, or the butterflies and moths. They're not even close to each other, are they?”

“True! But I think it's weird to have hundreds of parents. And the sky and sea weren't very nice to you. Maybe we don't have parents.”

“That feels weird. Do you think we could still be brother and sister without parents? Because that feels more right than ‘friend’. And, I don't know what other words would work.”

They had been referring to each other as friends for a good while by then. Neither spoke to others often, but when they did, they would always speak about each other. 

The butterflies seemed happy to hear about the life of one of their gods, but the moths seemed terrified of Hope’s friend. 

“I think that works,” Hope hums, “We can be brothers, or sisters. How do you know which one to use?”

“I kind of just do, you know? It's almost instinct.”

“Huh, yeah. But what are we?”

They don't answer that last question for a good while still.

~

“Welcome to our humble land,” the priestess says reverently. 

Hope thinks it's funny, because she's in their realm instead. It's not like they could leave, anyways, even if they can see into the waking like Fear does. 

“I had a question,” they say, “What is a parent? And how do I tell when someone is a parent?”

The priestess looks surprised. 

“Well, my lady, a parent is someone who raises you. A mother would have birthed you, too, and a father would be her husband.”

“So, someone who's given birth is a mother?”

“...In a sense, yes.”

~

“So I think we don't have parents, since nobody really gave birth to us. Probably,” Hope tells their newfound brother. 

“Hmm. Well, that makes sense. Who needs parents anyways?”

“Yeah! Not us, that’s who!”

And so their little family stayed that little, and nobody was around to mind them.
